Watch4Sat Privacy Policy
Effective date: 2026-07-31

Watch4Sat is an English-only, standalone Wear OS satellite pass tracker. It has
no accounts, ads, analytics, marketing SDK, developer-operated backend, or
cloud sync. It does not sell personal data.

LOCATION

Watch4Sat needs a station location (QTH) for pass, pointing, distance, Doppler,
map, and alert functions. You can enter a location, choose a map point, or
explicitly request the watch's location. GPS prefers Google Play services
Fused Location Provider and falls back to Android framework providers when
fused location is unavailable or does not return an acceptable fix. The app
does not request background location. An accepted station location and
coordinate-derived pass snapshots are stored locally on the watch.

Watch4Sat does not send saved coordinates to a Watch4Sat server. Google Play
services and Android may process location as part of providing a requested fix
under their own privacy policies.

NETWORK SERVICES

The watch contacts CelesTrak for orbital elements, SatNOGS DB for transmitter
metadata, and OpenStreetMap for online map tiles. Providers can receive the
network address, request time, URL, and Watch4Sat User-Agent.

OpenStreetMap tile URLs contain zoom and tile coordinates. They reveal the
approximate viewed area and can reveal the approximate area of a saved or
viewed QTH. Normal tiles may be cached locally. Watch4Sat does not bulk-download
OSM tiles. Offline world maps use a packaged Natural Earth asset and make no
map-network request.

LOCAL DATA

The watch stores the station, settings, selected satellites, cached public
orbital and transmitter records, refresh metadata, pass snapshots, alert
state, notification/alarm configuration, and ordinary OSM tile cache.
Notification surfaces can show satellite names, pass times, and alert details.

Cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for all app data.

DIAGNOSTICS

Watch4Sat has no remote crash reporter or analytics. Local Android system logs
can contain location-provider names, timing, fix age, accuracy, and result
state. Watch4Sat intentionally excludes latitude and longitude from these logs.

DELETION

The in-app clear-location action removes station fields, coordinate-derived
pass snapshots, pass-alert state, scheduled pass alarms, and derived in-memory
station/pass state. It does not remove public satellite/transmitter caches,
other settings, or ordinary OSM cache.

Use Wear OS application settings to clear Watch4Sat storage, or uninstall the
app, to delete all Watch4Sat data on the watch. There is no server account to
delete.

PERMISSIONS

Location is used after an explicit location action. Notifications and exact
alarms support enabled pass alerts. Network access supports data refresh and
online maps. Boot completion restores applicable local alert scheduling.
